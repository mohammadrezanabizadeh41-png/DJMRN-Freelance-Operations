Concept study 01 · Demo only

Corporate presence

A fictional concept for presenting a company's services.

Concept project
Problem
A fictional service studio needed to explain varied offers without hiding the first action.
Design approach
We separated the promise, service categories and contact preview, then built a restrained responsive hierarchy.
Original artifact
An original vector composition showing a service-page grid, navigation and an editorial hero.
What this concept teaches
Clear content pathways should come before decorative motion.

Concept study 02 · Demo only

Creative portfolio

An illustrative way to showcase independent creative projects.

Concept project
Problem
A hypothetical independent maker needed to give selected projects context beyond a grid of thumbnails.
Design approach
We organized each story around intent, design decisions and an accessible route to explore more.
Original artifact
An original vector storyboard with project tiles, annotation and navigation states.
What this concept teaches
A credible portfolio explains decisions; it does not invent clients or results.

Concept study 03 · Demo only

Digital catalog

A mock experience for finding and comparing digital kits.

Concept project
Problem
An imaginary collection of digital kits needed transparent categories and an understandable choice process.
Design approach
We sketched distinct category cards and simple comparison cues without implying a real checkout.
Original artifact
An original vector catalog interface study with filters and product cards.
What this concept teaches
Honest demo labels and clear exclusions matter more than fake conversion numbers.
