- `Freelance` describes contractual employment type, not fixed-duration or fixed-pay status. `Online since 18 days` means when the listing was published, NOT delivery time.
- Keep Workplace=Remote, Contract type=Freelance, Worldwide, Newest. User's actual saved daily alert must not be changed without permission. This only narrows type/location, not the duration.
- **Try a single literal keyword at a time in main search bar:** `Festpreis`, `einmalig`, `fixed price`, `one-off`, `Pauschalpreis`. Results may not interpret synonyms/quote matching consistently, and absence of word does not prove a contract is monthly; record count and IDs for each query.
- Practical title keywords with task signals: `WordPress website`, `landing page`, `bug fix`, `website migration`, `logo design`, `Canva templates`, `video editing`, `Python script`, `API integration`. These DO NOT guarantee one-off; verify description.
- For German job cards look for `Festpreis`, `einmalig`, `Pauschalangebot`, `Werk`, `klar definierte Lieferung`. Avoid automatically treating `Projektbasis` or `kurzfristig` as guaranteed single-delivery; they may still be ongoing.

## Real one-off shortlist criteria
1. Identifiable **single deliverable** and acceptance tests, timeline toward delivery not a staff-augmentation assignment.

3. Actual technical capability demonstrated; user residence eligible with truthful identity and permitted lawful payment; free application available.
4. Confirm client needs one-time handover, not recurring support/ongoing hiring, before preparing any outgoing expression of interest.
