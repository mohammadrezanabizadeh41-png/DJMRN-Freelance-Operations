### Legal and accounting context — do not overclaim
- German condominium ownership law [WEG §28](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/woeigg/__28.html) obliges the property manager to produce annual accounts and the asset statement; [WEG §29(2)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/woeigg/__29.html) indicates the owners' administrative advisory board should review annual accounts and provide its position.
- These sections alone **do not establish a universal mandatory external Wirtschaftsprüfer audit/certification for every WEG**. In this private listing, `testiert` could mean a formal professional opinion, an agreed signed review letter or another scope. The actual signing authority, standard of assurance, duties, liability insurance, licensing and availability from user's lawful residence must be clarified **before any commitment**. Do **not** assert CPA certification is legally always required, and do **not** promise that general accounting diploma qualifies to issue a testat.
