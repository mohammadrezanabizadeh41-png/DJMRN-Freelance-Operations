## Lessons and operational consequences
1. **Seven already in repo** — no new individual opportunity files for repeated cards. The original assessments remain in FMAP-032/033 and are only linked from this batch.
2. **Both truly new ads expressly fail duration gate:** Tenth Revolution CyberArk PAM >=6 months; Amoria Bond OSPlus QA >=12 months. User's permanent no-monthly/no-multi-month rule applies even if the component duties include finite reports or test cases.
3. The `100% remote` search card is not sufficient eligibility proof; CyberArk requires onshore Germany or nearshore Europe while user must use true current residence. Pay units and contract eligibility not assessed beyond exclusion.
4. Avoid treating [ad date Oct 15] or [start Nov 2026] as contract duration; length taken from public full original only. The Amoria OSPlus card's `12 months+` screenshot and public listing's `12 months` give minimum of approximately a year, not guaranteed exact end.
5. Bounded deliverable search recommendations remain: `WordPress Fehler beheben`, `WooCommerce`, `Landing Page erstellen`, `Excel Bericht erstellen`, `Python Skript erstellen`, `UX Audit`, `Video schneiden`. Full text must prove fixed independent handover, language, competencies, pay unit and lawful contracting/residency.
6. **No one-off shortlisted candidates, no PDF requests, no proposals, no client messages or payments; no alteration to saved filters; no Notion writes.**
