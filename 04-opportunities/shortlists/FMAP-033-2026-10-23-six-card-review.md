## Outcome and lessons
1. **Six cards, one confirmed repeat (SecurePoint), five distinct others, zero one-off application candidates.**
2. **Two definite 3-month+ SOLCOM jobs** — D365 and insurance UX. Build a prototype or plugin within a multi-month team placement does NOT convert role into a single independently handed-over job.
3. **Meerab IT Business Analyst** offers deliverable-like skills (BRDs, process maps, wireframes), but posting is a role description with no specified one-time acceptance, exact fee or fixed work scope. Duration UNKNOWN, not asserted monthly.
4. Sales offers have materially different pay models: Allianz hourly rate **or** fixed + success bonus subject to negotiation; Perspicuum fee UNKNOWN. Both unsuitable due continuous cold/warm outreach, **not** because all sales jobs necessarily commission-only.
5. Screenshot `100% remote` does not independently settle applicant residence eligibility, eligibility from user's true location, or lawful payment. SecurePoint €192k and Meerab €300 budget header figures are not verified fixed one-off payments.
6. Original SOLCOM project title references 1084432/1084429 and internal job-number metadata may differ; platform numeric IDs (#3061148/#3061100) are a **different numbering system**; preserve identity relationships.
7. No original Chrome PDFs, application, quota, direct-message, subscription, search preference or Notion changes. Public web review and GitHub operational archiving only.

**Next search strategy:** Stop sampling mainly corporate staff roles; try one bounded-output keyword at a time (`WordPress Fehler beheben`, `WooCommerce`, `Landing Page erstellen`, `Excel-Bericht`, `Python Skript`, `UX Audit`, `Figma Landing Page`), confirm actual independently accepted fee unit, deadline, deliverable and real skill/contract/payout eligibility. Reuse previous screen IDs instead of rereading duplicates.
