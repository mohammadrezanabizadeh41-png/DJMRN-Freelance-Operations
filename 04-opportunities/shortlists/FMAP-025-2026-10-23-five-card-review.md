## Lessons and next step
1. `klar umrissene Einzelaufträge` genuinely distinguishes **bounded per-assignment testing** from long engagements. Nevertheless general contractor recruitment is not proof of a specific, currently payable deliverable; require a particular task and scoped contract before counting as accepted.
2. Quality/regulated technical work has non-substitutable **experience, credentials, languages and live equipment**. Good task fit in theory does not warrant a premature application.
3. `100% remote` listing-card display may contradict body `hybrid` and `Remote-Anteil nach Absprache`. Check actual contract/locations, not badge.
4. **Screen results:** five cards; two repeats; three new; **zero viable candidates for PDF or bidding**. Thryve or FourEnergy could be *learning tracks* but do not mark as earning opportunities.
5. Search one at a time for modest bounded deliverables matching proven skills: `WordPress Fehler beheben`, `Landing Page erstellen`, `Excel Bericht`, `Python Skript`, `Video bearbeiten`. Check fee unit, legal/residency and payment path. Do not change saved site filters or alerts without user's instruction.
