## P-A v1 — 20-second International Brand Teaser: “Less Switching. More Living.”

**Core proposition (hypothesis):** AI assistance for both work and everyday life, not a specialist-only CRM or calendar product. One spoken or typed request may coordinate multiple connected actions; actual tool success is not shown until observed.
**Audience:** individual person with everyday + professional schedule; not exclusively entrepreneurs/freelancers. No fabricated speed study, unsubstituted $ savings, guaranteed task execution, product UI or sales market.
**Primary goal:** G1 Brand Awareness. **Secondary:** G2 site exploration. **CTA:** “Explore Orsola” → `orsola.ai` on-screen at end.
**Format:** 20s 16:9 master; 9:16 recompose if/when producing. **Tone:** warm, premium, approachable, agency-grade—not corporate-only and not a robot-heavy sci-fi trope.
**Claim guard:** words “can help”, “designed to” and “bring together” rather than “instantly automates everything”; user review when illustrating consequential sends.
