# ORSOLA-AD-LANE-001 — Universal simplicity, time, orchestration, voice and value vs alternatives

**Dated:** 2026-10-23 (Europe/Berlin). **Status:** PUBLIC_FIRST_PARTY_RESEARCH + EVIDENCE_TIERS + CREATIVE_HYPOTHESES; NOT PRODUCT/PRICE PERFORMANCE-TESTED.
**User-requested new advertising lane:** demonstrate Orsola for ordinary people, families, students, professionals and businesses—not solely founders and freelancers. Sell clear outcomes (a helpful day), natural-language/voice interaction, cross-tool orchestration, reminders and recurring planning; research competitor promises and cost complexity honestly.
**New dimensions:** `X1_ONE_CONVERSATION`, `X2_LOWER_COGNITIVE_SETUP`, `X3_VOICE_FIRST`, `X4_FOLLOW_THROUGH`, `X5_WORK_LIFE_BRIDGE`, `X6_VALUE_VS_APP_STACK`.
**Coverage:** Cross-cutting overlay on G1 awareness, G2 website, G3 registration, G4 activation, G5 subscription, G6 return use; prior 32 O01–O32 preserved as separate idea IDs.
**Key caution:** Natural-language convenience is a **vendor-described feature**; “requires zero configuration”, “saves X hours”, “always works autonomously”, “cheapest”, “unique/only AI capable of this” and measurable reduction in costs are **NOT VERIFIED**. These may only become ad claims after fit-for-purpose independent testing.

**Anti-misinformation:** Simply naming six competing subscriptions and adding their advertised prices to claim Orsola is cheaper is an invalid comparison. Several alternatives are free for some use cases, credits differ fundamentally, plans are per-seat vs per-user, and account/tool limits differ. Competitive pricing must be reverified on publication day and compared at **same workload and deployment conditions**.

## 3. Why "simple for a nontechnical person" must be tested
- **Onboarding requirements:** account, OAuth, tool selection, time zone, credit allowance and optional permissions are not zero-configuration. Complexity can differ across services.
- **Natural spoken input:** voice UI is documented, but microphone permissions, audio credits and feature availability affect ease and cost.
- **Automation vs reminders:** a task can run on a schedule within limits; some external actions may need approval; do not imply Orsola independently executes every multi-app operation without user supervision.
- **Time:** “fewer tab switches” is a qualitative illustration; measured fewer clicks and time saved require a benchmark with comparable successful output.
- **Cost:** “one useful subscription across needs” is a positioning hypothesis, not mathematical elimination of other subscriptions.
- **Accessibility:** don't imply voice interface is medically accessible for all users or works perfectly with all accents without tests.

## 4. Controlled benchmarking proposal — only after permission to use real products
**Benchmark test set:** `T01` draft client email and check free time, `T02` weekly calendar digest + reminder, `T03` meeting recap→follow-up, `T04` receipt→expense categorization, `T05` user voice to scheduled reminder.
**Controls:** same dummy dataset, same device/network class, same instruction in chosen language, same calendar and approved account sources, same number of repetitions (suggested n=5 per workflow), comparable plan and feature entitlement, normalized setup notes, human-verified completion, errors and edits.
**Metrics (not yet measured):**
- `SETUP_MINUTES`: first successful connection/permission workflow.
- `ACTIVE_USER_SECONDS`: time actually clicking/typing/reviewing, excludes waiting for AI but show `ELAPSED_SECONDS` separately.
- `UI_INTERACTIONS`: clicks/tabs/apps or form fields, but only when doing identical task.
- `SUCCESS_RATE`: human-verified task completed correctly, not whether model claimed success.
- `HUMAN_CORRECTIONS`: manual edits/confirmation operations.
- `ACTUAL_MONTHLY_COST_FOR_FIXED_WORKLOAD`: plan charge + credit overages + provider/dependent service fees (same usage), billed period/currency normalized.
- `LANGUAGE/VOICE/PRIVACY` failures and logs.
Do not benchmark rivals by reproducing unauthorized customer data, breaking service terms, buying without consent or conflating free trial with permanent free plan.

## 5. Creative expression ladder (risk controlled)
- **LEVEL-1 now:** “A simpler way to ask.” “Email, calendar and reminders in one conversation.” “For the days that don't fit in one app.” Use conceptual motion, label illustrative footage where needed.
- **LEVEL-2 after authorized real demo:** “Ask Orsola to check availability and prepare a reply” with truthful app footage. “A weekly overview at the time you choose” if an actual run succeeds.
- **LEVEL-3 only after measured comparison:** “X fewer steps vs [specific configuration] for workflow T01.” “X% less active user time in our n=... test.” Include test method, endpoints, limits and comparisons in ad footnote or linked evidence.
- **REJECTED without exceptional proof:** “world's first/unique”, “no technical knowledge whatsoever”, “zero setup”, “instant guaranteed action”, “cheaper than every rival”, “all tasks at no cost”, “saves 10 hours each week”, “one subscription replaces everything”.

## 7. Source-backed advertising narrative lessons (not Orsola performance data)
- [Google ABCD guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551) says to attract, brand early, connect human relevance and direct to one CTA; separate creative tactics for awareness vs action.
- [OKAD 2026 AI portrait app case](https://okad.agency/cases/creative-testing-ai-app-meta-ads/): agency reports structured creative testing over 160+ videos with multiple creators/hooks; illustrates *format and hypothesis experimentation*, not verified superiority of "simplicity" scripts or predictable results for Orsola.
- [HubSpot AI-led video case, 2025](https://www.thecompassvideo.com/work/brand-ai-ads): self-reported 71% greater brand lift, 46% less cost/lifted user vs traditional approach. Production method uses storyboards and generative plates + **traditional UI animations**. Its reported figures are not Orsola forecasts; independent methodology unavailable here.
- [Microsoft Advertising x dentsu, July 2025](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/resources/discover/case-studies/how-dentsu-is-accelerating-creative-workflows-by-6x-with-generative-ai-in-microsoft-advertising-platform): case reports AI accelerated certain *creative production* tasks up to 6X; **NOT** a proof Orsola speeds users up or an ad claiming it would save 6X time.
- [Google official creative instructions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551) prioritize product-specific demonstration in action-stage ads and early brand in awareness.
