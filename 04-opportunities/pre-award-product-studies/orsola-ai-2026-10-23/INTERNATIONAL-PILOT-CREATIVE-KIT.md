One **global English picture-lock master** with reusable interface/text layers + one **short vertical** concept. Narration and caption files are separate for future FR, ES, AR; do not generate dozens of redundant locale videos until the brand selects two directions. No client's credentials, real mailbox or other users' names. No samples uploaded to public site/repo before owner authorizes release.

**Goal:** viewer sees one meaningful safe action, with choice and review. **Required before real-video claim:** an authorized Orsola demo on synthetic accounts, captured without secrets.
| Sec | Live-demo checklist / shot | English VO |

## Creative platform cut list — proposed not contracted
- MASTER: 1920×1080, 25 or 30 fps after client standard chosen, 20–40s.
- SHORT: 1080×1920, text center-safe in platform overlay regions. YouTube recommended 9:16 and <60s; use **10–30s** for action-oriented vertical.
- 1:1 or 4:5 only if specified by accepted publisher placement; require recomposed graphics.
- Captions as editable SRT + burnt-in sample (no official language equivalence implied); music and sound effects licensed, voice consent and performer release if needed.
- Distinguish HOOK A vs B vs C by first three seconds only to preserve proof: problem ("Too many tabs?"), outcome ("One request, next steps."), review ("Your tools. Your choice.").
- No inferences of numerical time savings, conversion rate, worldwide sales availability, native app release, AI accuracy, privacy certification or real partner endorsement.

## Localization template and QA
- **English (master):** neutral international tone, avoid US-exclusive colloquialisms unless a US-only cohort selected.
- **French:** rewrite for brevity and accurate command grammar; consider on-screen sentence length.
- **Spanish:** neutral international Spanish first, then adapt to target region if proven eligible.
- **Arabic:** true right-to-left shaping, mirroring layout only where it preserves brand/UX meaning; proofread mixed Latin product name.
- **Persian or any additional language:** possible creative-only future request; no inference of market eligibility, product Persian interface or approved country targeting.
- For all languages, human/native review or owner approval before commercial release; optional silent-with-caption variants for mobile feed.
