## 1. Foundational proven publisher guideline — Google ABCD
[Google Ads official: ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en)
- **Attract:** first frames matter, distinctive contrast, attention-getting but truthful hook.
- **Brand:** show brand early and reinforce throughout; don't hide the name until only the closing frame.
- **Connect:** keep one clear problem/emotional relevance; characters and relatability when useful.
- **Direct:** a visible and narrated CTA with a single target action, distinct by funnel goal.
These are published *best-practice recommendations*, not a guaranteed formula or substitute for a controlled A/B test.

### Creative principle
For AI SaaS, use **generative video for cinematic metaphors and transitional b-roll**, not as an authority for exact logo text, user interface, billing displays or independent verification of claimed product performance. UI, logo, prices, disclaimers and subtitles should be composed as editable vector/text/screen layers during editing.

## 7. Repeatable six-goal competitor-ad research protocol (no ad creative copied)
For each G1–G6:
1. Pick comparison categories and labels: Lindy (AI agents), Fyxer (mail+meeting), Motion/Reclaim (tasks/calendar), Notion AI/productivity or similarly relevant primary sources. Brands are **categories for research**, not proven exact competitors of equivalent features.
2. Inspect 3–5 ads/category in [TikTok Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads) where available; tag country, language, objective, publishing date, ratio, duration, hook first 3 seconds, brand timestamp, scene count, CTA, actor/UI ratio, captions, objective and *any source-visible* stats. Access/coverage may be limited without login.
3. Search same brands in [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) and [Google Ads Transparency](https://adstransparency.google.com/). Don't infer paid strategy or ROI from existence of ads.
4. Save **ad URL, date, public performance metric if displayed, analyst note**, never a protected full competitor video in public GitHub. Mark `PERFORMANCE_UNOBSERVED` unless actual publisher metric is available.
5. Convert a **mechanism** (e.g. dynamic captions + real demo), never a third party's exact composition/copy/likeness/logo, into our own concept.
6. Form a single testable hypothesis: X hook vs Y hook for **one** audience, goal, CTA and single landing link. Only say `WINNER` after actual data, not intuition.
7. Review 2026 trends monthly or before any real paid launch; don't treat present style as permanently optimal.

## Final methodological judgment
Prioritize **high-quality art direction + actual Orsola interface when documented + precise editing/voice/captions** over unstructured long text-to-video generations. Sample work can proceed with site assets and conceptual animation; product-capability claims must wait for synthetic live test or clearly labelled simulation.
