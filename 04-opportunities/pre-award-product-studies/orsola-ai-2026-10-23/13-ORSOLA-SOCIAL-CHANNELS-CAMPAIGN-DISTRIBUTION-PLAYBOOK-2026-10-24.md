### Organic, owned, partner and paid channel plan with reporting/automation controls

**Version:** 2026-10-24 / proposed campaign framework. **No Orsola social placement, paid ad buy, creator partnership, pixel installation, publishing automation or experiment is asserted to have been activated.** Source policies/pricing/eligibility must be refreshed for any real publication.  
**Companion:** [English case study](11-ORSOLA-CLIENT-PRESENTATION-CASE-STUDY-EN-2026-10-24.md) · [Persian client report](12-ORSOLA-ADVERTISING-STRATEGY-AND-PRODUCTION-REPORT-FA-2026-10-24.md) · [Existing objective research](STAGE-4-MARKET-AND-CAMPAIGN-VALIDATION.md) · [Prior catalog](STAGE-5-ADVERTISING-OPPORTUNITY-CATALOG.md).

## 1. Channel strategy starts with measurable intent, not number of social accounts

Use **Goal → Persona → Verified customer problem → Evidence-backed copy → Native format → Landing/action → Measurement → Learn/revise**. Distribute to channels that reach that persona; do not blindly post identical generic exports everywhere. Choose one campaign goal per test (awareness, relevant site visits, eligible registration, meaningful first use, subscription, re-use).

**Six defined campaign objectives:**
- G1 brand reach and memory: recognize "Orsola" and its general AI-assistant role;
- G2 website/feature exploration: engaged visit to validated feature page;
- G3 valid signup: completed signup within eligibility and policy;
- G4 activation: independent human-verified first useful approved workflow;
- G5 paid conversion: completed purchase at correct plan/region;
- G6 retention/referral: subsequent meaningful use, authorized recommendation.

For G3–G5, do not activate location-dependent calls to action or payment pages before owner confirms live country eligibility, product state and legal terms.

## 4. Hook strategy, messages and CTA testing

**Three possible test axes, changed one at a time:**
- **Problem hook:** “One day. Too many tabs?” / overwhelmed workday;
- **Outcome hook:** “What if everything felt connected?” / single list concept;
- **Control/voice hook:** “Just ask. Review what matters.” / transparent voice and user approvals.

**CTA by goal:** Explore Orsola (G1) · See Features (G2) · Start Free (G3, eligibility verified) · Try One Workflow (G4, tested) · Compare Plans (G5, fresh prices) · Set a Reminder (G6, verified schedule).

**Avoid:** “no training required for anyone”, “all tasks run autonomously”, “all countries served”, “guaranteed hours saved”, “more effective/cheaper than every alternative” without controlled data.

**Research repositories:**

- [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/): actual advertiser creatives and placements, not guaranteed proof of return.

- [Publisher creative methods](AD-CREATIVE-METHODS-REFERENCE-RESEARCH.md): case-study analysis; Holded (LinkedIn), The Dairy / Faye / Air Arabia / Panteon (TikTok) *publisher-reported* results, not Orsola results.

For each competitor creative: save `public URL, date, format, audience, first-3s hook, brand reveal timing, live actor/UI balance, music/captions, CTA, platform-visible metric and reliability caveat`. Reuse storytelling principles, not third-party protected footage, exact ad text, personality or unlicensed music.

**Testing:** hold landing page/budget/audience constant while changing one hook, determine decision threshold before running, compare click intent and actual conversion, beware small sample sizes and platform auto-optimization confounding the result.

## 8. Distribution automation *design*, no deployment

Automation possibilities **after owner authorizes accounts and policies**:
1. **Content register:** approved MP4, picture aspect, caption locale, thumbnail, text, legal claims approval, license, version, checksum and approved landing URL.
2. **Scheduling:** official native schedulers or permitted connected publishing service; drafts → owner review → schedule/publish. No bots bypassing platform permissions.
3. **UTM builder and event schema:** campaign/source/medium/content variant, analytics only with consent/legal approval.
4. **Performance pull:** retrieve aggregate permitted stats by campaign daily/weekly; avoid private user identifiers in a public repo.
5. **Learning feedback:** flag meaningful changes (rejected ad, poor brand clarity, falling watch-through), human decides new test/creative variant.
6. **Security:** platform roles, access tokens managed outside repo, rate limits, revocation, moderation, advertising disclosure and geographic eligibility; no publishing on user identity without explicit authorization.

**Nothing in this plan authorizes autonomous publishing, spend or paid subscriptions.**

Use a content-level measurement line: `ASSET_ID, VARIANT, PLATFORM, LOCALE, AUDIENCE, OBJECTIVE, CTA, LANDING, PUBLISH_APPROVAL, START/END, AD_COST, IMPRESSIONS, VIEWS_3S/6S, COMPLETES, QUALIFIED_CLICKS, ACTUAL_CONVERSIONS, ATTRIBUTION, NOTES`. No fabricated ROI.

## 10. Required gates before sharing or live campaign

- Current official site and current original Orsola logo SVG; wordmark and domain **typed by editor**, never trusted from generated video.
- Approval of factual features, app status, eligible geo and subscription offer.
- Authorized source videos, screenshot/privacy redaction, licenses, commercial rights for AI-generated images, video, voice, SFX/music, as well as proof of no restricted watermark.
- Final video mastering and SRT: actual MP4 duration/frame rate/aspect, music ducking around speech, captions synced from actual voice waveform; accessible non-music watching.
- Owner approval of landing, captions, paid spend, tracking pixels, automation tools and upload destinations; after release track outcomes and make iterative creative improvements.

**Interpretation note:** This is the campaign/distribution capability being *proposed* based on documented research, not a record of paid campaigns executed by DJMRN for Orsola.
