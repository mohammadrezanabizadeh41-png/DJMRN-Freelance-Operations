2. Upload the **specifically approved shareable media only** to an owner-controlled shared-drive location or public GitHub folder/LFS/release if authorized and rights allow. Keep originals immutable; include thumbnails or licensed compressed copies and SHA256.
3. Update this directory with **actual, tested URLs** and `PUBLIC/PRIVATE/SHARE_GRANTED` state. Do not expose private Library aliases, user data, signed URLs, tokens or logins.
4. Verify source clip dimensions/durations, merged export, official SVG overlays, watermark/attribution policy, English VO/SRT and sound copyrights.
5. Send [English case study](11-ORSOLA-CLIENT-PRESENTATION-CASE-STUDY-EN-2026-10-24.md) + actual **approved playable clip** + one link to this index; optionally provide [Persian operational detail](12-ORSOLA-ADVERTISING-STRATEGY-AND-PRODUCTION-REPORT-FA-2026-10-24.md) and [campaign playbook](13-ORSOLA-SOCIAL-CHANNELS-CAMPAIGN-DISTRIBUTION-PLAYBOOK-2026-10-24.md).
