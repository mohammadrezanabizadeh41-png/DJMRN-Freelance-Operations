# Orsola AI — Stage 3 commercial advertising creative brief

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** DRAFT_NOT_APPROVED / NO_VIDEO_PRODUCED
**Owner:** [pre-award case](README.md). Technical/marketing claim basis: [PRODUCT-RESEARCH](PRODUCT-RESEARCH.md).

## Strategic recommendation (judgment)
Explain **"from a request to a reviewed, connected result"** rather than claiming that Orsola is the most powerful AI, replaces employees or never requires human review. It is a vendor-promoted workflow benefit, not tested product performance.

**Default narrative:** one request across Gmail/Outlook + calendar and a clear draft-to-review stage.
**Audience hypothesis:** US/Canada founders/solo operators, professionals and freelancers; target only where official terms permit.
**Potential direct response CTA:** Start Free (free plan limited to monthly credits; confirm pricing, eligibility, signup and landing page at publication).
**Message draft:** “One AI. Your tools. Your day, handled.” (brand owner needs to approve final adaptation and slogan).

## Evidence-to-claim traffic-light register
**Candidate with demo:** Gmail+Calendar in one conversation; preparing email reply; task reminders and meeting summaries; user review of sensitive mail sends. Docs: https://www.orsola.ai/en/features and https://www.orsola.ai/en/support/connections/email .
**Conditional and freshness-sensitive:** 1000 monthly free credits; $3/$5/$8 beta tiers; supported language claims; phone availability; target country/age/credits; any app store icons.
**No-go without fresh objective evidence/legal sign-off:** "saves X hours," "best AI," "instant 100% success," "certified GDPR," "no data ever leaves Orsola," "unlimited free video," "apps downloadable now," "available all over the world," "replaces your team," "makes any change without risk."
**Never show:** real private email, calendar, payment details, unconsented recorded speakers, protected brand asset without rights, fake UI outcome passed off as measured success.

## User journeys with lower/higher risk
1. **Main hero**: email → calendar → prepared draft. Tight scope, authoritative sources, can test with synthetic data.
2. **Secondary**: meeting consent → transcript → action list. Requires microphone/consent and acceptable transcription.
3. **Optional personal**: reminders/expense categorization. Avoid claims about medical advice, food safety or financial decisions.
4. **Defer**: Phone Agent, nutrition medical/allergen protection and AI video cost promises until separate entitlement/security/regional tests.

## Minimum test plan before production
- **T01 real UI:** in licensed sandbox, connect minimal-scope test Google/Microsoft accounts; no personal or client secrets.
- **T02 positive:** sample customer email identified, synthetic calendar availability correctly read, draft produced; screenshots and timestamps for each.
- **T03 permission fail:** no connected email → transparent error, not hallucinated success.
- **T04 conflict:** busy calendar should not recommend occupied slot.
- **T05 action gate:** ambiguous send and clear send both handled per documented consent; no unintended outgoing email.
- **T06 plan/quota:** correct package entitlement, media generation quote and credit sufficiency confirmed.
- **T07 locale:** UI language vs response language documented; no promise of Persian UI.
- **T08 launch/market:** product access and allowed audience/CTA match current Terms and live Download.
- **T09 legal:** person likeness, voice, brand logo, music, software screenshot rights documented.
- **T10 accessibility/export:** readable subtitles, mobile-safe UI text, aspect ratios, scene pacing, final CTA.

**Any failed core demo, missing commercial right or unknown cost = no factual screen-demo claim.** Use labeled conceptual animation only if acceptable to client.
