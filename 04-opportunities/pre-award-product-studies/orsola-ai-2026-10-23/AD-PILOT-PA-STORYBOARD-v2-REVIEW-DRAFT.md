## 2. v2 editorial changes from historical v1

1. The original 0–6 second two-part explanation is condensed to a single 0–3 second opening; the authentic brand logo enters approximately **3 seconds**, rather than depending on a logo appearance after 6 seconds.
2. The spoken draft is reduced to **28 whitespace-delimited words** across 20 seconds (roughly 84 wpm as a simple total-length average, **not** a measured VO read). This leaves some pauses and a more legible brand end card; a real narrated read-through is still untested.
3. `S07` supplies visual logic (dispersed apps → one conversation); `S01` supplies human relevance (adult's day including family and work). We do not show “automation success” or actual authenticated product UI.
4. Clear up the factual claim: Orsola is introduced as a *personal and professional AI assistant*, and the depicted email/calendar/reminder categories are sourced from its public website; the moving cards are labeled `Illustrative concept`.
5. The closing logo/URL/CTA gets **three full seconds** in the recommended horizontal storyboard; “Explore Orsola” points to an informational website, NOT a country-specific signup/purchase action.

## 6. Art direction / composition rules

- **Brand:** use current public Orsola style approved as a *source baseline* by user: provisional purple `#7133D2`, CTA `#663399`, dark `#1B1627`, pale `#F8FAFC`. Contrast and vector identity must be inspected against original CSS/SVG before actual compositing. Plus Jakarta Sans was observed as website typography, subject to valid licensing.
- **Three technique bands:** `cinematic human` (PA01/PA03/PA05); `restrained 2.5D/conceptual UI` (PA02/PA04); `kinetic typography` (PA01/PA04/PA05). Not three separate style directions or sequential unrelated films.
- **Pacing:** 0–3 problem, 3–5.5 brand reveal, 5.5–9.5 human context, 9.5–14.5 conceptual association, 14.5–17 relief, 17–20 brand/CTA hold.
- **Sound:** English narrator (not yet selected/recorded), gentle rhythmic sound design and one audible but not aggressive brand cue; captions must remain readable without sound.
- **Aspect:** draft master 16:9, subject near center-safe for later vertical alternative; actual platform-safe zones depend on final channel, so 8/10% are creative working margins, not certified publisher specifications.
- **Privacy, data and copyright:** generic icons; no identifiable real emails/calendar entries/third-party logos; no cloned voices or licensed songs until rights checked; neither generated motion nor manually illustrated chat cards are genuine Orsola software.
- **Commercial:** no claim of $ savings, N minutes saved, zero setup, guaranteed connected app action, paid subscriptions in every country or globally downloadable iOS/Android app.
