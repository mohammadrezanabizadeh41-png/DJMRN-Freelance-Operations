# ORSOLA-017 — Storyboard creation method, ten visual treatments, selected Model07 and visual revision trace

**Recorded:** 2026-10-24 · **Status:** `ARCHIVAL_METHOD_AND_DECISION_TRACE`. This is a reconstruction of operational method from existing persisted GitHub/Library receipts and user conversations—not a claim of direct access to the private/hidden generating model internals nor a verbatim old-chat transcript.

## Purpose
Recover the complete *how we worked* process so a future creative team can explain, reproduce, critique and improve a 30-second AI-generated ad—not just see a folder of final images. Preserve provenance, versioning and distinction between storyboard pictures, AI-generated video and final post-production.

## A. Creative source → storyboard selection process

1. **Evidence-first positioning:** public website `https://www.orsola.ai/en`, public features, supports, logo SVG links, brand palette and product caveats were inventoried (see [product research](PRODUCT-RESEARCH.md), [brand registry](AD-MASTER-DATA-AND-ASSET-REGISTRY.md), [marketing strategy](ADVERTISING-STRATEGY.md)).
2. **Campaign brief:** selected goal G1/P-A, introducing an AI personal/professional assistant in a relatable day-in-the-life scenario. Separate campaigns G2–G6 remained unimplemented ideas, not claims in this sample. Human problem → orchestrated digital tools → relief → brand reveal.
3. **Narrative/options lab:** compared 12 story concepts, 12 hooks and 16 visual advertising techniques; user wanted nontechnical work-and-home applicability, attractive cinema and restrained UI. See [scenario lab](AD-SCENARIO-LAB-UNIVERSAL-USE-CASES-AND-COMPETITOR-INSIGHTS.md), [methods](AD-CREATIVE-METHODS-REFERENCE-RESEARCH.md), [creative matrix](02-CAMPAIGN-SCENARIO-ASSET-AND-PROMPT-MATRIX.md).
4. **First textual storyboards:** 20s six-scene P-A v1/v2/v3 drafts with timecodes; user selected English Hook A exact: `One day. Too many tabs?` within 0–3s. Other VO copy not yet final.
5. **Changed scope to full 30-second visual exploration:** produced **ten independent reference poster layouts**, each conceptually enumerating all 30 one-second frames (5 columns × 6 rows per original poster). Each single large PNG poster contains 30 thumbnails; **it is not 30 separately editable full-resolution still photographs**.
6. **Visual style comparison:** user chose **Model 07** among ten alternatives for hybrid cinematic human performance + vivid violet ribbons/tech/UI + kinetic typography. Model number is a creative treatment ID, **not** an AI foundation model or guaranteed Dola model setting.
7. **Storyboard decomposition:** new source panels for A 01–10 (00–10s), B 11–20 (10–20s), C 21–30 (20–30s) on three 5×2 contact-sheet PNGs. A/B/C designed as a continuous visual universe: same human, wardrobe, room, windows, lighting, purple/white style, sequential floating cards, end title.
8. **Fine-grained correction and preservation:** first 16 frames user-reviewed and combined; 13–16 explicitly show six floating digital cards clustering into ONE six-row organized list. Frames 17–20 happiness/child/golden sunlight candidate, rather than definite approved pixel-level locked storyboard. Part C original and revised separated; former ending with female in final seconds rejected, replacement violet light → logo + domain → city/mountain sunset explored.
9. **Visual production adapted to platform:** Dola text chat was reported unable to take image/storyboard files for model conditioning. Instead each physical action, camera direction, identity, site/logo text, SFX and timing was re-described in English text-to-video prompts. End scene changed from original revised 30-frame poster `logo then final sunset` to later confirmed **`sunset first, finished brand lockup last`**.
10. **Final delivery method (pending technical review):** individually generated scenes → actual video inspection → join and timing conform → voice over ≤25s target if 30s master → sound/music + actual aligned SRT → authentic logo replacement → final export. No embedded generator VO/music requested.

## D. Visual continuity card for storyboarding/prompts

- **Woman:** one visibly consistent woman, approx 28–32, fair/light natural skin, slim/oval face, expressive eyebrows, brown eyes, natural nose/lips, dark brown messy bun + a few loose strands, oversized beige/cream knitted sweater. User's final generated clips exhibited a recognizable woman but screenshot comparisons do **not** establish biometric identity, exact model consistency or cross-shot photo lock.
- **Set:** modern apartment/home office with large full-height city-facing windows, golden sunrise/sunset and lavender ambient light, clean desk, large desktop monitor, keyboard, ceramic coffee cup. The actress, computer geometry and sunshine direction should not jump across edits.
- **Brand:** Orsola appears on a clean white/purple computer homepage; `orsola.ai` official domain. A hollow/tilted glowing violet elliptical ring is **storyboard interpretation**; exact corporate mark requires official SVG overlay.
- **Effects:** floating productivity cards and badges are diegetic/promotional CGI concepts, not faithful app screenshots. Six connections progressively confirmed, then one list, then less clutter and warm real-life peace.
- **Sonic progression:** pressure sounds → per-item confirmation sounds → quiet room and coffee → outside ambience → delicate brand glow/chime; narration and commercial licensed music intentionally separated.
- **Negative conditions:** no fake live product successes, no other adult characters, no accidental different URL, no strange typography, no cartoon look, no generated lip-audio, no unsubtitled branded text in unrelated locales.

## E. Reproducible storyboard workflow for another advertising project

1. Validate target product identity and exact claim sources; date sources.
2. Choose ONE campaign objective and audience; write a single-sentence story arc.
3. Write a **timecoded story in text** first (hook / problem / action / outcome / CTA), separate factual action from conceptual CGI.
4. Generate 3–10 **distinct** visual treatments with stable IDs and grid contact sheets (30 × 1s for 30s).
5. Show all treatments to owner; record explicit selection; don't presume image approval from generation.
6. Convert selected style into A/B/C panels and correct characters, CTA timing, camera directions and scene-to-scene transitions.
7. Pin immutable original images and hashes; version revision panels rather than overwriting them.
8. Create a continuity card and accurate endcard brand assets (original SVG and text overlay).
9. Map storyboard beats to the actual video generator's supported input: image-to-video only if its **chosen UI truly accepts references**; otherwise text-only prompts detailed enough to recreate staging but clearly marked approximate.
10. Capture prompt text, result/screenshots, settings, model, duration and unknowns; audit every scene before joining.
11. When the story order evolves during production, **version the newer approved creative sequence separately** and conform the VO/SRT timing against actual final footage.
12. Approve licensed voice, sound, captions and distribution separately.
