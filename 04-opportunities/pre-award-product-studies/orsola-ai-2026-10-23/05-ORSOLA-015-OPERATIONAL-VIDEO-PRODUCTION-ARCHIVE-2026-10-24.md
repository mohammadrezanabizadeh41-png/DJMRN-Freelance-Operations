## 2. Production story and final scene intent

**User-chosen sequence:** 
1. **Opening / scene 1:** golden/pink-violet sunrise, fast cinematic push toward the modern apartment, stressed woman at desktop, many floating work/app notices, phone ring and beeps around seconds 2–4; camera approaches monitor, cursor hovers over the `Start with Orsola` button. Keep Orsola-branded white/violet screen, not generic website.
2. **Connecting / scene 2:** same beige-sweater woman and large windows; purple energy exits monitor, connects separate floating cards sequentially, visually illustrative green check and synchronized soft cash-register-style `cha-ching` confirmations, all cards consolidate into *one* clean list. No actual account execution verified.
3. **Relief / scene 3:** organized list, beautiful golden sunlight, visibly calmer and energetic same woman at keyboard with warm latte/cappuccino; refined single-ear aviation-style boom headset and natural silent lip movement demonstrate possible voice interaction; brief mother-and-child walk outdoors; final warm sunlight. No generated dialogue/music.
4. **Outro / scene 4:** begin outside near park/open city vista, reverse camera movement pulling out and back to reveal sunset mountains + skyline; transition to near-black, dispersed purple mist/ribbons converge into selected tilted hollow violet ring, then exact `Orsola` and `orsola.ai`; finish *on brand lockup*, not another sunset.
5. **Brand facts:** official name is case-sensitive `Orsola`, domain exactly `orsola.ai`, reference public homepage `https://www.orsola.ai/en`; official SVG URLs are in [asset registry](AD-MASTER-DATA-AND-ASSET-REGISTRY.md). AI-generated on-screen letters/UI can be inaccurate—use true SVG + manually set typography in edit.

**Stable cinematic continuity:** realistic woman approx late 20s/early 30s, light/fair skin, softly slim/oval face, brown eyes and expressive eyebrows, dark-brown hair in messy bun with loose strands, oversized cream/beige chunky knit sweater. Morning/golden light from full-height windows, home-office desk, large desktop monitor, warm modern city view. Character preservation via textual descriptions was mitigation, not true reference-lock evidence. No extra adult characters, no wardrobe swap, no overly cartoon/anime treatment.
