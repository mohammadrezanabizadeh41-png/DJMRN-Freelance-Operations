The previous 3 phases studied **what the product claims**, the web structure and a preliminary advertising creative direction. ## Funnel and measurement design — proposal, not analytics evidence
1. **Discovery:** eligible impressions and 3-second views/hold rate; ask whether product awareness exists.
2. **Interest:** qualified landing visits and CTA clicks; do not count likes as paying customers.
3. **Signup:** completed valid beta registrations (product owner analytics required).
4. **Activation:** completed first meaningful workflow (example: successful authorized connected action with user-reviewed output); exact event must be defined by product owner.
5. **Retention:** return to a meaningful feature after e.g. week or month; window must be selected and tracked, never invented as existing.
6. **Willingness to pay:** plan selection, valid purchase or explicit surveyed interest; not inferred from free registration.

**Measured baseline:** NONE. **No traffic, conversion, churn, CPA/CAC, ROAS, retention, LTV, signups or A/B test results were retrieved.** No claims of expected ROI or optimal acquisition channel permitted.
