## Executive summary

Rather than begin by generating arbitrary product footage, I built a **repeatable, evidence-led advertising workflow** for Orsola. The work began by mapping its official web presence, documented capabilities, message boundaries and available brand resources. I then designed a full-funnel campaign framework, explored consumer-facing everyday situations and competitor messaging, compared visual storytelling methods, built multiple timed storyboards, selected a consistent creative treatment, and developed/refined actual video-generation prompts with sound and scene continuity instructions.

The creative argument is simple: **people experience a fragmented day, not a list of software features.** Our flagship story moves from noisy email/calendar/task cards to a single orchestrated flow and then to a calmer everyday life. Visual effects communicate the *idea* of connected assistance; when showing real functional proof, live product demonstrations and owner approval are essential.

## 1. Product and audience positioning

Orsola publicly positions itself as an AI personal and professional assistant working across chat/voice, email, calendar, documents, tasks and reminders. The website emphasizes one conversation coordinating multiple tools and human confirmation when needed. See [official homepage](https://www.orsola.ai/en), [features](https://www.orsola.ai/en/features) and [support](https://www.orsola.ai/en/support). These are **first-party feature descriptions**, not our independent proof of reliability.

**Core creative angles:**
- **Simplicity for nontechnical users:** demonstrate a clear prompt and human-understandable outcome; avoid unsupported “zero setup” claims.
- **Workflow continuity:** email → availability → draft for review, or meeting → follow-ups; distinguish illustrated concept from authenticated workflow proof.
- **Work–life relevance:** family planning, freelancing, personal reminders and small business admin.
- **Voice-first utility:** show calm spoken interaction where the platform supports it; respect permissions and user confirmation.
- **Value versus fragmented tool subscriptions:** compare actual successful tasks, credits, limits, pricing, setup cost and human intervention; don't promise to replace every competitor or be universally cheaper.
- **Trust and control:** permissions, confirm-before-send, privacy and review gates made visible, not hidden.

**Audience segments designed:** independent professionals/freelancers, founders/operators, knowledge workers, everyday organizers/working parents, cautious privacy-conscious prospects, paid-plan evaluators. English master copy designed for prospective French, Spanish and Arabic *creative localization* where approved; localization does not imply country-specific product eligibility.

A campaign is **not just a video**: each pilot can contain hook variations, 16:9 website/YouTube version, 9:16 social cutdown, caption/SRT variants, landing page, CTA, A/B methodology and an objective-appropriate metric.

## 3. How the selected P-A film was made

**Preproduction:** official site/brand inventory → creative brief and 12 scenarios → 12 alternative first-three-second hooks → six-pilot strategy → 10×30-frame storyboard style boards → decision and selection of Model07 → revisions of A/B/C visual panels → detailed per-scene English prompts → realistic recurring character/room/website rules → render trials.

**Visual story:**
1. **Overload:** reverse sunrise-to-apartment push-in; a recognizable young woman at a sunlit desk overwhelmed by digital cards; phone/notification sound effects; Orsola website introduced.
2. **Connection:** Orsola-inspired violet light travels from monitor across floating email/calendar/task/meeting/document cards, progressively marking them green, with soft checkout-style confirmation chimes, until they coalesce into **one unified list**.
3. **Relief and voice:** same woman in same sweater and room, realistic warm sunlight, coffee, subtle single-ear headset/voice-interaction gesture; briefly walking with a child outdoors.
4. **Brand outro:** camera pulls outward from park/city to golden mountain sunset (inverse of opening), then violet luminous particles condense on black into a ring/wordmark and **exact** `Orsola` + `orsola.ai` held as the final frame.

**Production adaptation:** Dola's user-facing chat/video interface did not reliably accept storyboard image references, so descriptive text-to-video prompts were developed. Some attempts visibly failed; later user-visible screenshots corroborated the first/second scene direction, and the creator reports the picture generation stage is complete. Because prompt models can alter faces, logos, words and clip lengths, validation plus genuine SVG/name compositing are essential. See [the prompt ledger](06-ORSOLA-VIDEO-PROMPTS-EXECUTED-AND-REVISIONS-2026-10-24.md) and [render observation log](08-ORSOLA-VIDEO-RUN-RECEIPTS-AND-LESSONS-2026-10-24.md).

**Picture/video evidence:** selected storyboard PNGs and images were backed up in the ChatGPT project Library with named manifests and checksums, not attached as downloadable GitHub binaries; [asset directory and custody](14-ORSOLA-PROJECT-EVIDENCE-ASSET-LINK-CATALOG-2026-10-24.md) explains retrieval. ## 4. Creative strategy and competitor research — what informs our differentiation

The research looked at publicly documented strengths of **Lindy** (agents/cross-tool), **Motion** and **Reclaim AI** (scheduling), **Fyxer** (email/meeting), **Zapier** (workflows) and **Calendly** (booking). The purpose was **category positioning**, not a misleading assertion that any competitor cannot perform basic Orsola capabilities. Full comparators, dated source links and fair same-workload test method: [research](AD-LANE-SIMPLICITY-TIME-VALUE-COMPETITOR-RESEARCH.md).

Potential differentiated campaigns can test:
- Clear “one request, multiple tools” narrative using real verified handoff clips;
- A voice-first routine versus a text-heavy setup flow;
- Everyday personal + professional scenarios instead of only enterprise automation;
- Human review and consent controls as a trust signal;
- Equivalent task outcomes versus monthly cost/credits/interaction counts. **No claimed productivity gain or pricing superiority before measurement.**

Inspiration/evidence sources include **TikTok Creative Center / Top Ads**, **Meta Ad Library**, **Google Ads Transparency Center**, publisher video-creative guidance and dated provider case studies. Published example outcomes from other brands are **not predictions** for Orsola and no actual Orsola ad-performance experiment was run.

## 5. Proposed audience acquisition and distribution

**Owned & organic:** official website hero/feature pages, tutorial/article clips, YouTube videos and Shorts, LinkedIn professional stories, Instagram/Facebook Reels, TikTok native everyday-life videos, brand social teasers, founder/influencer collaborations, community/FAQ explainers, launch emails and product onboarding content after consent. The goal is an interconnected content program, not random cross-posting.

**Sponsored (only with explicit owner budget/territory approval):** YouTube/Google video campaigns, Meta Ads Reels, LinkedIn sponsored video and TikTok Ads; test one audience × one message × one CTA × one landing page at a time. Retargeting, consent/cookies/region, approved product claims and accurate tracking must be verified before activation. **No budget, platform campaign or automated posting has actually been executed.**

**Format variants:** 16:9 longer film, 9:16 short/Reels/TikTok/Shorts, and square/4:5 for selected placements. Opening brand/problem message within the first seconds; editable subtitles and safe zones; action and landing page aligned with objective. The [distribution plan](13-ORSOLA-SOCIAL-CHANNELS-CAMPAIGN-DISTRIBUTION-PLAYBOOK-2026-10-24.md) includes channel-specific rationale, sequencing, metrics and automation approval gates.

## 6. Voice, edit, captions and final deliverables

The next step is to confirm the **actual picture runtime**, assemble the cuts and select/approve the English script. The most recent proposed narration is approximately **23 seconds**, featuring a calm, mature low-register male voice and the opening `One day. Too many tabs?`. [TTSMaker](https://ttsmaker.com/) has been **identified as an option**, not independently used to produce a final VO file for this case study. Exact syllable pacing, Orsola pronunciation, silence/cue positions, license and commercial use should be checked during production.

**Postproduction to complete:** one mastered visual cut; authentic logo and domain overlay; time-measured voice WAV; independent music/SFX mix and ducking; English SRT measured from real audio; aspect-ratio adaptations, rights/policy compliance, QC and export. The 30-second historical storyboard and “VO last word ≤25s” are **planned targets** and must be reconciled with the actual rendered picture—no final exported duration is asserted.

## 7. What a client can review / engagement proposal

**Available documented deliverables:** research and web-source inventory; official brand asset references; competitor-positioning logic; six campaign briefs; twelve narrative concepts and twelve hooks; sixteen creative methods; ten storyboard style boards plus selected Model07 design and revisions; four detailed film-scene prompt families and run/QA lessons; initial English VO copy; campaign measurement/launch blueprint.

**Next proposed commissioned scope (requires agreement):**
- Approve campaign goal, audience/country eligibility, messaging, claims and approved assets.
- Review and conform sample footage; refine brand-authentic visuals and an accepted endcard.
- Finalize voice, audio and subtitles; deliver an approved 16:9 review version and/or 9:16 short-form cuts.
- If desired, create a small, instrumented paid/organic A/B test with consent-based tracking and report observed—not invented—results.

**Decision-quality principle:** research → hypothesis → human-focused concept → storyboard → prompt/test → compositing → claims check → deliverable → measurement. We can supply creative assets and planning before any account access or ad spend; publishing and distribution require separate owner authorization.
