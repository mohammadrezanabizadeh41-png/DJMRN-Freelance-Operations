The creator asked for a **low, warm, mature/middle-aged male English voice with calming, confident delivery** for ~23 seconds of brand-video picture.

## 1. Exact proposed English promotional narration

```text
One day. Too many tabs?

Meet Orsola, your AI assistant for work and everyday life.

Emails, meetings, and reminders—together in one conversation.

Less switching. More clarity.

More time for what matters.

Orsola. From conversation to completion.
```

**Language:** English only in final ad; user-directed copy, but still an **editorial proposal**, not final brand approval. **Pronunciation:** test `Orsola` with the selected voice and get preferred official pronunciation if available. Avoid unintentionally naming a competitor or implying outcomes the product could not demonstrate.

## 2. Target voice character / render settings — starting suggestions, not measured presets

| Dimension | Preferred quality |
|---|---|
| Speaker | Adult/middle-aged male-sounding English narration, not impersonation or cloning of any identifiable real individual |
| Tone | Deep low register, resonant, warm, calm, trustworthy and quietly reassuring |
| Accent | Neutral/international English; US English is a reasonable *test* for this English ad |
| Speed | Natural unhurried ~0.90× initial speed if provider allows, then measured adjustment |
| Pause | 0.3–0.5s between major sentences as necessary to actual target, avoid unnatural long silences |
| Emotional arc | Slight urgency/question in opening → curious relief as connecting cards appear → warm human optimism with woman/family → memorable understated brand close |
| No generation bed | Generate *clean voice only*: no background music, phone rings, synthetic ambient, reverb-heavy processing or third-party voice likeness |
| Best archival output | WAV or highest available clean audio from provider; if only MP3, preserve original and derive a separately named mastering file |

**Duration is estimated, not guaranteed.** In the previous conversation a rough **23-second** voice target was requested; actual length depends on selected model/voice, speed, expressive pauses, pronunciation and export. Final delivery must sync to the **true assembled MP4**. The older storyboard was originally 30s with voiced syllables ending ≤25s; a modern ~23s VO may fit if picture actually accommodates it. Do not create captions based only on rough timecodes.

## 4. Tool selection and current known evidence

**User wanted a free web service with selectable male voice:** [TTSMaker official website](https://ttsmaker.com/) was recommended in chat as a possible fit, and its [official commercial audio terms](https://ttsmaker.com/copyright_and_commercial_license_terms) are publicly available. **That is not a receipt proving the Orsola script has been converted to audio.** Current free tier quotas, export format and feature availability should be checked again on day of use; avoid surprise charges.

**Editor candidate:** [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve) (free variant or another suitable NLE), to assemble picture, mix narration and SFX, correct Orsola SVG/wordmark and import/edit subtitle tracks. Auto-transcription features can be paid-edition-only; do not assume free Resolve automatically generates accurate captions.

**SRT workflow:** TTSMaker's public interface at time of inspection advertised an SRT export option for generated audio, but verify quality and correct timing against **real waveform**. Alternatively create SRT manually or with an approved separate alignment tool. Final SRT must reflect **spoken** words, not original unrecorded script estimates.

**Track assignment** (proposed):
- V1: finalized joined Orsola picture;
- V2: official logo/website exact typography compositing and/or on-screen captions;
- A1: clean English low/mature male voice;
