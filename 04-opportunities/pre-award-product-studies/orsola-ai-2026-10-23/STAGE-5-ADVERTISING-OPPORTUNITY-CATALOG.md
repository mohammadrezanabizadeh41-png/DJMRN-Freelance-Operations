**Purpose:** practical library of *possible one-off freelancer deliverables* to discuss with the product owner, NOT a list of actual paying job posts and NOT a promise to execute all 32. Every candidate must be a bounded project with delivery and approval.

## Shortlisting method
Each opportunity has:
- **P1:** initial shortlist: first-party capability documented, visually intelligible, relatively low regulated-claims burden, possible one-off deliverable. Still **requires safe live demo and brand permission** if product screens are shown.
- **P2:** valid after the first demo, audience objective/analytics or owner instructions; no claim of empirically greater ROI.
- **P3:** high-risk due to unverified app availability, permissions, limited plans, health/phone compliance, testimonials or enterprise claims.

Scores P1–P3 are **editorial readiness judgments**, NOT measures of demand, CTR, ROAS, CPA, CPM or pay rate. `Demo` = authorized product test with dummy content. `Claims` = company sign-off and source fresh at release.

## How to decide between 32 candidates next
1. Owner chooses **one primary business objective**: eligible awareness / beta signup / first successful activation / repeat use / paid conversion.
2. Owner chooses **one eligible persona and geography** (current terms US/CA), and **one platform** where they can lawfully distribute.
3. Check rights and **actual workflow evidence** for that persona.
4. Choose lowest-risk P1 asset requiring least production; give exact scoped deliverables, runtime, formats, voice, timeline and revision allowance.
5. Agree in advance on one measurement event + how analytics provenance will be accessed without leaking customer data.
6. Only then make script/storyboard, show drafts for brand/client approval, generate/edit, QA/export and deliver. Publication and ad spending require separate explicit authorization.
