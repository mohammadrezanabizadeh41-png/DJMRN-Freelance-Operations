## 1. Eight structures to test across SaaS/AI teasers
- `N01 SCATTER→CONVERGE`: familiar proliferation of tabs then visual orchestration.
- `N02 ONE SENTENCE`: human spoken request, visible relevant steps, calm end.
- `N03 EVERYDAY PARALLEL`: same need in personal and professional settings, diverse ages/adults/lifestyles.
- `N04 BEFORE/AFTER WITHOUT NUMBER`: different cognitive loads without measured time/ROI claims.
- `N05 THREE SMALL WINS`: show 3 independent relevant tasks, not 13 feature logos.
- `N06 HUMAN REVIEW`: transparent preview/confirmation step makes actions credible.
- `N07 FIRST SUCCESS TUTORIAL`: close-up user prompt→correct checked result; real app proof.
- `N08 VALUE EXPLAINER`: group categories as one interaction, transparent credit plan and no unsupported cost comparisons.

**Non-negotiable:** script must contain ONE primary claim/goal; 2–3s hook → relatable user problem → plausible tool path → outcome WITH evidence → brand/CTA. This is research hypothesis, not guaranteed winning structure.

## 3. Four detailed ready-to-review *script treatments* (EN drafts, not outputs)

### S01 — “Monday, Without the Mental Load” — 25s
**Human problem:** A normal person juggles appointments and family-related tasks; not a full-time CEO.
- 00–04: kitchen counter with three analog colored sticky notes, a laptop, a neutral calendar. **VO:** “A new week. A hundred little things to remember.”
- 04–08: adult looks at laptop and speaks a concept request: `Every Monday morning, help me see what's coming this week.` **VO:** “What if you could simply ask?”
- 08–16: animated planner cards gently connect to schedule overview, labelled `Illustrative concept`. **VO:** “Orsola is designed to bring plans, reminders, and everyday tasks into one conversation.”
- 16–21: calmer breakfast scene with an optional follow-up card; no asserted time saving. **VO:** “Less switching. More room for your day.”
- 21–25: approved exact wordmark, URL, `Explore Orsola`.
**Test gate:** official recurring task instructions support requesting a weekly digest, but real future delivery has not been verified. No actual child or family health data.

### S02 — “One Message, Next Steps” — 35s
**Human problem:** service professional receives email while juggling a calendar. 
- 00–05: synthetic client email alongside busy calendar; **VO:** “One client question can turn into five separate steps.”
- 05–10: neutral finger on keyboard / optional voice input; **VO:** “What if you could ask for the next step?”
- 10–22: **ONLY IF RUN** true controlled product UI: `Read my latest sample client email, check Thursday afternoon, draft a reply.` Distinguish step-wise reading and tool permissions.
- 22–28: an accurate reply draft appears **for review**, not "sent" automatically. **VO:** “Your details come together. You review the result.”
- 28–35: exact wordmark and product URL, `See Orsola in action`.
**Test gate:** real demo mandatory, authorization/permission and correct calendar; controlled no unintended sends.

### S07 — “Three Apps, One Thought” — 20s
**Human problem:** many ordinary people have a scattered digital day.
- 00–03: fast three-tab abstract montage, satisfying SFX, `Inbox. Calendar. Reminders.`
- 03–07: hand moves to one neutral question; abstract apps morph into one purple timeline; **VO:** “Your day doesn't happen in one app.”
- 07–14: graphic journey of a question connecting icons (clearly conceptual), no real UI inferred; **VO:** “Meet Orsola. One conversation across the tools you choose.”
- 14–17: person at leisure or with friend; **VO:** “More clarity in the everyday.”
- 17–20: wordmark `orsola.ai`, `Explore Orsola`.
**Gate:** ideal for **brand sample**; use own icons not competitor copies.

### S12 — “The Price of Too Many Tools” — 30s or 45s / gated
**Human problem:** a freelancer/individual considering tools with overlapping feature categories and different price plans.
- 00–05: separate cards labelled scheduling, meeting summary, inbox, reminders; **VO:** “How many subscriptions does your workday really need?”
- 05–14: introduce Orsola's documented category coverage; transparent user-controlled tool selection; **VO:** “Explore how one connected assistant could fit more of your routine.”
- 14–25: OPTIONAL factual plan comparison *only after* verified same-task benchmark; unapproved version shows labels `See plans and credit limits` rather than numeric “X dollars saved”.
- 25–30: `Explore plans` / domain. **Avoid** competitor brand logos, fictitious prices, user time saved, global checkout promise.
**Gate:** ad becomes quantified competitor comparison only after equal workloads, credited entitlements, actual user time and cost.

- Query `Lindy`, `Fyxer`, `Motion`, `Reclaim`, `Zapier` and relevant personal assistant categories in [TikTok Top Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/inspiration/topads), [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/), [Google Ads Transparency](https://adstransparency.google.com/) by allowed country, platform objective and date.

- Compare ad **narrative mechanisms**, not copy/performer's voice/music/face/composition. Two ad samples using tabs-to-calm do not prove Orsola is unique.
- Marketing vendor results are potential inspiration but not independent efficacy studies. Examples: [OKAD AI app test](https://okad.agency/cases/creative-testing-ai-app-meta-ads/) reports its 160+ video test portfolio and acquisition targets; [HubSpot AI brand ad production story](https://www.thecompassvideo.com/work/brand-ai-ads) reports self-measured brand lift and UI compositing; neither proves Orsola outcomes.
- No actual specific competitor creative/video downloaded, reproduced or used here; **COMPETITOR_AD_CAPTURE_STATUS: NOT_PERFORMED**. Only official product positioning and published campaign case methodology researched.

Evaluate concept on `A hook recognizability`, `B broad-audience relatability`, `C feature proof feasibility`, `D brand fit`, `E editability/production cost`, `F compliance risk`, `G clarity of CTA` rated 1–5 **only after real team review**.
