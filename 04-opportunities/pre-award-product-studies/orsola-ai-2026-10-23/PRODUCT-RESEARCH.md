## Readout of critical discrepancies

**U01 — native apps:** https://www.orsola.ai/en/download says Coming soon, while https://www.orsola.ai/en/support/start/apps-and-devices talks about mobile releases. DO NOT assert store publication until confirmed.
**U02 — Video plan:** Video agent can be selected on Free per https://www.orsola.ai/en/support/video/make-a-video but Pricing shows image/video generation excluded from Free and Plus. Hypothesis: available planning UI ≠ entitlement to AI generation, but not proven.
**U03 — Google Drive scope:** latest connected-file help describes broad Drive permission; other previously retrieved help reference describes smaller scope; verify actual OAuth screen with non-personal test account before marketing privacy claims.
**U04 — Gmail actions:** page versions differ on whether archive/read/trash manipulation is supported; avoid promising complex email housekeeping.
**U05 — action safeguards:** mail send review documented. Terms warn that previously configured workflows can execute without repeated per-instance approval. Avoid absolute claim 'always confirms every action.'
**U06 — localization & geography:** en/ar/fr/es UI ≠ worldwide sales. Terms currently restrict offering/targeting to US/Canada.
**U07 — data protection:** security/privacy descriptions are not a third-party security audit; the Features text suggests GDPR status but the Terms and privacy territory clauses do not establish offering to EEA. Legal review before 'GDPR compliant' advertising.
**U08 — AI fidelity:** no objective tests, guaranteed speed, accuracy, uptime, time saved or cross-agent correctness evidence.
**U09 — media rights:** public images/logos and software screenshots remain owner IP; commercial use needs owner permission.
**U10 — crawler discrepancies:** Firecrawl Crawl returned 25 generic 404 among 103 English URLs; direct re-scrape recovered at least one. Treat as extraction coverage uncertainty, not proof site has 25 broken links.

## Source-verified product boundaries
- Phone number and calling are region restricted and incur recurring/usage charges; emergency calling unsupported.
- Meeting transcription/recording of others needs informed consent and can fail with wrong audio/mixed-language settings.
- Nutrition is wellness/food tracking, not diagnosis or drug advice.
- Video Studio can generate short ad concepts and slideshows, but final rendered output and cost have **NOT** been tested or selected as production pipeline.
- Data protection claims require vendor/legal sign-off; Microsoft Azure US and listed third-party processors are described by company.
- All USD plan amounts/date on this page are transient snapshot of 2026-10-23 public beta.
