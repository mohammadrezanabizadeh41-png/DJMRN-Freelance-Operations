# LESSON-2026-004 — One-off deliverable can still be unsafe to accept when certification and software experience are essential

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23 · **Case:** [BBNI WEG annual charge statement #3064264](../04-opportunities/OPP-20261023-005.md). **Evidence:** 1-page PDF checked; public WEG §§28–29 and Immoware24 documentation; no live account access or client interaction.
**Observation:** Client defines a finite one-time job (27-unit 2025 Hausgeldabrechnung, already booked in Immoware24) and shows a €700 budget. But expressly requires **Immoware24 knowledge and past annual WEG account audit experience**, and a `geprüfte und testierte` result with undefined assurance/signatory standard.
**Rule:** Passing **ONE_OFF_STRUCTURE** is necessary but not sufficient. Before committing, verify **professional aptitude and demonstrable hands-on software experience**, right to issue named attestation, documents/data permissions, liability, acceptance, actual fixed price, deadline, client eligibility and lawful payment.
**Do not generalize:** WEG law §§28–29 do not automatically establish that every annual condominium account requires an external CPA/Wirtschaftsprüfer certificate. `testiert` is a term the **client** used, but its intended legal level cannot be decided from ad alone. Do not describe absence of certified German tax license as categorical statutory disqualification without further evidence.
**Decision:** Format suitable, immediate application **on hold** because mandatory prior experience unverified; no user final accept/reject or bid. Learn Immoware24 separately, but do not imply a tutorial creates the practical experience demanded.
**Data-security lesson:** WEG financial source records may contain owner and bank personal data; never commit them to private project GitHub or send through AI systems without explicit client authorization, controls and necessity.
