# LESSON-2026-002 — Matching technical keywords is not a scoped freelance order

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23 · **Evidence strength:** one verified PDF, plus first-party company/contact cross-check.
**Source job:** [OPP-20261023-003](../04-opportunities/OPP-20261023-003.md), freelancermap #3064457.
**Scenario:** searching only for one-off, fixed-deliverable, finite tasks while a card features matching AI/RAG/agents/Python tags.
**What happened:** full 2-page listing calls for networking with experienced freelancers; future assignments depend on client need; employer requests profile, availability and hourly rate. There is **no active discrete job** with known deliverable, schedule or price.
**Reusable heuristic:** classify such adverts as TALENT_POOL/NETWORKING_LEAD, not an actionable ONE_OFF_JOB. Only promote to bid shortlist if actual defined output, deadline/scope and pricing/payment terms are supplied. Many relevant technology tags ≠ proved fit or available work.
**Counterexample/limit:** hourly work can be a one-time project and future leads may become fixed-scope assignments; do not claim this company will *only* offer long-term jobs. Label duration UNKNOWN.
**Outcome:** Avoided using a potentially limited application slot on an unscoped lead; no client work performed and no bid submitted.
