# LESSON-2026-003 — "Budget" at job card can be daily rate, and later PDF pages reveal duration

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23 · **Status:** VERIFIED_ON_ONE_REAL_LISTING (do not extrapolate to every freelancermap job).
**Case:** [NovaSearch #3064452, OPP-20261023-004](../04-opportunities/OPP-20261023-004.md). Four-page PDF SHA256 and source permalink are in OPP record.
**Scenario:** searching fixed-scope one-off work. Result card showed `600.00 € Budget`, `Start date 1/2027`, `100% workload` and `100% remote`.
**Source-backed finding:** p4 of full description says `Tagessatz 600 Euro` = daily rate, NOT €600 fixed job fee; `Projektlaufzeit: 01.01.2027 bis 30.06.2027` = six-month period. No single handover order and no confirmed net payout.
**Practice:** Never rank earnings/time from the card's **Budget** field alone. For each apparently attractive listing verify unit of compensation (`Stundensatz` hourly / `Tagessatz` daily / `Festpreis` fixed), `Auslastung` capacity, `Projektlaufzeit` contract term and actual payment conditions in **all PDF pages**. Dates like `1/2027` on a card may only be start date. Skip verified multi-month work under this user's rule.
**Limitations:** One listing proves one source/display mismatch, not a general defect or misleading intent of every ad. Day-rate engagements may be valid freelance work for other users; rejected here because of explicit user preference.
**Outcome:** Rejected without consuming any verified application slot. No confirmed account action and no actual contracted/paid delivery.
